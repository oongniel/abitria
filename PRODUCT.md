# Sillage — product context

> Inferred from the brief and the owners' spreadsheet (`new stock.xlsx`); no interview was run. Assumptions are marked.

## What it is
A batch-based inventory and profit tracker for a small perfume resale business. Stock is bought in the UAE (costs in **AED**) and sold in the Philippines (prices in **PHP**) through resellers who keep a fixed commission per bottle. Each purchase run is a **batch**, funded by partners, and profit is judged per batch.

## Who uses it
The business owners (two partners in the sample sheet). *Assumption:* mostly on a phone while messaging customers, sometimes on a laptop when buying a new batch.

## The one job
"How is this batch doing, and what's left to sell?" — answered in seconds, then record a sale in two taps.

## Domain rules (from the sheet)
- less commission (net) = retail − commission (300 PHP)
- cost in PHP = cost AED × rate (17)
- margin value = net − cost PHP; margin % = margin value ÷ net
- total sales = net × stock; converted back to AED ÷ rate
- net profit is split between partners (sheet: equally; app: by capital share, which is equal when capital is equal)

## Mode
Operate. Scanability, trust in the numbers, fast entry. Brand lives in details.
